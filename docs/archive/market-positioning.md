## PRICING POSITIONING

YS-MATRIX should position at the **value tier** — more expensive than Excel/freemium tools, but significantly cheaper than enterprise ERPs (CDK, Dealertrack, Oracle).

**Value Message:** "Enterprise features at a small business price."

**Price Anchoring:** Compare to:
- Monthly cost of a mechanic ($100-200)
- Cost of one missed installment payment ($500-5000)
- Cost of one lost vehicle record (priceless)
- Monthly salary of one admin employee ($500-1000)

**The ROI Story:**
- If YS-MATRIX prevents just one missed installment payment per year → it pays for itself
- If YS-MATRIX saves 2 hours of admin work per day → saves ~40 hours/month → worth 10x the subscription
- If YS-MATRIX helps identify one bad pricing decision → increased margin covers years of subscription
